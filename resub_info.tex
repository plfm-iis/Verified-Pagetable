The paper was previously submitted to the 20th USENIX Symposium on Operating Systems Design and Implementation (OSDI '26) on December 17, 2025.

In this revision, we have polished the writing throughout the manuscript to improve clarity and ensure that the concepts are accessible to readers. Additionally, we have expanded our discussion of related work to provide a more comprehensive background and explicitly detail the concrete limitations of prior approaches. Finally, to clarify our research's positioning and demonstrate its practical potential within the broader scope of full-system verification, we have formally integrated our framework with an existing verification tool, Spoq, to integrate our paging function proofs with the end-to-end functional correctness verification of a secure Linux-based hypervisor, SeKVM.
